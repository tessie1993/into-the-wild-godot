# Gear & Wearables — Into the Wild

> Status: design draft. Passive equips, no energy cost (economy §5). Permanent once crafted.
> Progression: C/U outer ring · R inner ring + bench · L turn 10+ with Crafting Bench.

| Item | Element | Tier | CE | Recipe | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grass Cloak | Sun | C | 3 | 2 Leaves + 1 Straw | Ignore first weather event each round |
| Bark Sandals | Wood | C | 3 | 2 Bark + 1 Vines | +1 move once per turn (jungle/meadow) |
| Traveler's Pack | Sun | U | 6 | 1 Flax (U) + 3 Vines | +2 backpack slots (economy §2) |
| Pearl Pendant | Water | U | 6 | 1 Pearl (U) + 3 Shells | +1 on creature befriend rolls |
| Spidersilk Greatpack | Wood | R | 18 | 1 Spidersilk (R) + 1 Flax (U) + 6 Commons (Vines/Bark) | +4 backpack slots |

| Titanbark Frame | Wood | L | 54 | 1 Titanbark (L) + 1 Ironwood (R) + 1 Spidersilk (R) + 9 Commons | +6 slots; Legendary cards take no slot |
| Mistheart Mantle | Sky | L | 54 | 1 Mistheart (L) + 1 Cloudsilk (R) + 1 Frost Lotus (R) + 9 Commons | +2 move; immune to weather & terrain slow |

**Notes**
- Backpack line (Traveler's Pack +2 → Greatpack +4 → Titanbark +6) follows independent tiering — craft from scratch, don't stack; equip highest.
- Gear slots per player: suggest 3 (head/body/feet or free-form) — TBD with player board design.
