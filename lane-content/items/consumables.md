# Consumables — Into the Wild

> Status: design draft. Single-use. Budget = 1× tier value (economy §4.1): C ≤3 / U 3 / R 9 / L 27 CE.
> Food fills the energy meter (cap 5) in Care phase; potions = one-shot effects, usable on your turn.

## Food (energy)

| Item | Element | Tier | CE | Recipe | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Herb Tea | Water | C | 2 | 1 Fresh Water + 1 Leaves | +1 ⚡; cure one minor ailment |
| Cooked Meal | mixed | C | 3 | 1 Fish + 1 Grain + 1 Stick (fuel, at fire) | +3 ⚡ (economy §5; needs Campfire) |
| Solar Feast | Sun | L | 27 | 1 Sunheart Grain (L) | **All players** +3 ⚡; cook gains +1 Light (group meal) |

## Potions & tonics

| Item | Element | Tier | CE | Recipe | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Healing Salve | Wood | U | 3 | 1 Medicinal Herbs (U) | Full heal / cleanse one negative status |
| Honey Tonic | Sun | U | 3 | 1 Honey (U) | +2 ⚡ and one free reroll this turn |
| Frost Lotus Elixir | Water | R | 9 | 1 Frost Lotus (R) | Freeze a creature encounter — skip its event entirely |
| Dreamspore Draught | Ether | R | 9 | 1 Dreamspore (R) | Peek top 3 cards of any deck; Learning action counts double this turn |
| Elixir of Dawn | Sun | L | 27 | 1 Dawnbloom (L) | Refill energy to 5 **and** take a second main action |

**Notes**
- Fork consumables (Nightshade → healing draught OR poison vial) live in `dark-kit.md` — one recipe, two outputs.
- Consumable recipes are deliberately 1-ingredient at U+ — the resource card *is* the cost; crafting action converts it.
- Elixir of Dawn's second action is the strongest single effect in the game — priced at full Legendary, playtest first.
