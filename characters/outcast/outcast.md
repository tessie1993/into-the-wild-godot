# Character: The Outcast

## Profile & Traits
- **Background:** Ship's stowaway and scavenger, accustomed to survival in harsh conditions, isolation, and rogue tactics.
- **Element Affinity:** ♥ Moon (Swamp), ✗ Sun (Meadow)
- **Base Move Speed:** Average.
- **Unique Signature Item:** *Thief's Prybar* — Unique craftable tool. Allows silent cache access and stealing from storage caches without triggering event alerts.

## Unique Skill
- **Survivalist Guile:**
  * Concept: Can set traps on hex tiles, navigate hazards safely, and scavenge discarded resources from other players.

## Bonuses & Perks
- **Swamp Dweller:** Ignores movement penalties and dark event hazards on Swamp/Moon tiles.
- **Scrapper's Eye:** Gains +1 Common whenever another player discards or drops an item due to setbacks.
- **Deep Pockets:** Advanced resource card hand size limit is **9 cards** (instead of 7).
- **Rage Capitalizer:** Gains $+1$ to all fight rolls when the Island Rage track is 6 or higher.

## Skill Tree Draft

### Generic Branch: Survivalist
*Shared with all castaways.*
1. **Scavenger's Eye (Common):** Gain +1 Common resource of choice when gathering.
2. **Sturdy Pack (Uncommon):** +2 backpack slots (increases carrying capacity from 6 to 8).
3. **Iron Constitution (Rare):** Meditating at camp costs 0 energy (free meditation).

### Unique Branch: Scavenger
*Unique to the Outcast.*
1. **Trapper (Common):** Craft simple snare traps that lock creatures or slow other players.
2. **Cache Raider (Uncommon):** Take cards directly from storage caches or shared depots without paying the standard Light cost.
3. **Shadow Stalker (Rare):** Avoid creature attacks entirely by shifting into adjacent hexes.
