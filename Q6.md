# VP scale and numeric victory thresholds for the three Ways

**Verdict:** NEEDS_WORK  ·  **Lane:** systems  ·  **Confidence:** medium  ·  **Platform:** both

**Conclusion:** Propose VP thresholds Way1≥14 / Way2≥7 / Way3≥16 with Light thresholds at the existing band boundaries (+3 / +8 / −8), built on a new small-scale VP faucet (1–8 VP per source, not the 3x CE ladder) — because the Hypocrisy Penalty (VP−Light≥5) only makes sense if VP and Light share an order of magnitude. Hand arithmetic gets Way1 and Way2 to comparable investment (~5 turns/~42 CE vs ~4 turns/~28 CE) but not exact parity; flag the Care-phase gift/trade lane as a structural loophole that needs sim verification before this can be called truly equal.

---

## The load-bearing finding first

**VP cannot be scaled like CE.** CE runs to the hundreds (baseline lifetime income 98.4 CE) because Common=1…Legendary=27 is a 3x ladder meant to span that range. Light runs **−10…+10**. Spec §5's Hypocrisy Penalty is `floor((VP−Light)/5)` energy lost — if VP is CE-scale (tens to hundreds) and Light is single-digit, that difference is *always* ≥5 for any player with real progress: the penalty fires on turn 3 and never stops. If VP were instead scaled to nearly nothing, the penalty would never fire and the rule would be dead text. **The two scales have to live in the same neighborhood on purpose.** That's the actual content of GDD Q6 underneath "pick some numbers" — and it also explains why `creatures.md`'s ad hoc quest rewards already sit at 1–5 VP: whoever wrote those was (correctly) feeling for this constraint without it being written down anywhere.

So: VP faucet is proposed in the **1–8 point range per source**, not the 1/3/9/27 CE ladder. Total realistic VP over a 15-turn Standard game lands in the **0–20ish** range — commensurate with Light's ±10.

## Proposed thresholds

| Way | VP | Light | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Capable | **≥ 14** | **≥ +3** | just crosses into the "Light" band (3–7) |
| 2 — Enlightened | **≥ 7** | **≥ +8** | "maxed-out" read as entering the "Max Light" band (8–10) — same threshold the Guardian Gate itself uses — rather than the literal +10 peak |
| 3 — Dark | **≥ 16** | **≤ −8** | entering "Max Dark" (−10 to −8), the same point `duality.json` already exempts from Hypocrisy |

Reading "maxed-out" as *band entry* rather than the literal endpoint is a judgment call stated explicitly here so it can be overridden — it's what makes Way 1/2 parity possible at all; requiring the literal +10 makes Way 2 measurably more expensive again (see below).

## Reachability arithmetic (15-turn Standard game)

Baseline pacifist economy from `sim/montecarlo.py`: 98.4 CE over 12 income turns (7 gather + 3 explore + 2 creature) → **8.2 CE/turn average**, leaving **3 spare turns** out of 15 (this 15−12=3 gap is where quest/craft/magic/learning/give-back-light turns come from — not separately documented, derived here).

**Way 1 build** (private goal 3 + Guardian step-1 2 + Hard quest 4×2 + Easy quest 1 = **14 VP**; 1 kept befriend + Guardian giveback = **3 Light**):
- Turns: Guardian step (craft+turn-in, 2) + 2× Hard quest (2) + Easy quest (1) = **5 dedicated turns** → 3 from the spare pool, 2 converted from income turns (12→10).
- Material CE: 6 (Guardian craft) + 9×2 (Hard quest deliverables) + 2 (Easy) = 26 CE, plus 2×8.2 = 16.4 CE opportunity cost → **≈42 CE total investment**.

**Way 2 build** (Give-Back-Light×3 + Easy quest 1 + private goal 3 = **8 VP** reached, comfortably clears the 7 bar; 2 befriends + 3× Give-Back-Light + 1 creature-quest bonus = **9 Light**, clears the 8 bar):
- Turns: 3× Give-Back-Light (3, fits the whole spare pool) + Easy quest (1, converts 1 income turn 12→11) = **4 dedicated turns**.
- Material CE: 6×3 + 2 = 20 CE, plus 1×8.2 = 8.2 CE opportunity → **≈28 CE total investment**.

**Ratio: 1.25x the turns, 1.5x the CE for Way 1 vs Way 2.** Not exact parity, but the same order of magnitude, and both are achievable inside a 15-turn game with room to spare — which is the meaningful bar ("statistically equal" can't be verified to the CE without an actual quest-aware sim; see risk below).

**Way 3 build** (Dark Hard×3 + Dark Medium×2 = **16 VP**, at the 1.5x premium; Max Dark band forces creatures to auto-attack, so the F2 "always-fight" income of 107.4 CE applies without the player choosing it):
- Turns: 5 dedicated turns — same count as Way 1.
- Material CE: (9×1.5)×3 + (3×1.5)×2 = 40.5+9 = 49.5 CE, plus 2×8.95 (fight-adjusted CE/turn) = 17.9 → **≈67 CE total investment**, **1.6x Way 1's cost for +14% more VP.**

That's the shape spec §6 asks for: VP requirement only *slightly* higher than Way 1 (16 vs 14), but the underlying economics (1.5x quest tax, forced isolation from Guardian-chain and group-quest VP routes) make it substantially pricier — "slightly harder in fact," with the *feel* gap supplied by things already in canon and not re-priced here: mandatory fights, Rage-driven `+F` creature difficulty, the discard penalty at Rage≥5, doubled negative effects in the Max Dark band, and losing every group-quest/Guardian-blessing option (spec §11: "you are alone").

## Hypocrisy Penalty check (the ask: does it fire sensibly?)

`floor((VP−Light)/5)` energy lost, computed at the proposed thresholds:

| Case | VP − Light | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| Way 1 finish (14, +3) | 11 | **−2 Energy/Care phase** |
| Way 2 finish (7, +8) | −1 | **0** (Light exceeds VP) |
| Way 3 finish (16, −8) pre-exemption | 24 | would be −4, but Max Dark is `hypocrisy_exempt: true` in `canon/duality.json` → **0** |
| Ordinary mid-game player (turn 8, VP≈3, Light≈2) | 1 | **0** |
| A player who dumps every spare turn into VP quests and does zero Light actions (VP≈20, Light 0) | 20 | **−4 Energy** |

It fires *sensibly*: never for a normal player, moderately (−2) for someone finishing Way 1 exactly at its own bar, not at all for Way 2 (its own high Light exempts it structurally, not just by the band rule), and hard (−4) only for the deliberate hypocrite the rule is named for. This only works because VP was kept in the same 0–20 neighborhood as Light's ±10 — confirms the scale choice above rather than being a separate result.

## Open risk this doesn't resolve

**Care-phase gifting is a free lane for Light that VP has no equivalent of.** `gift_card` (+1 Light) and `generous_trade_3ce` (+1 Light) fire in the Care phase, which is bonus-only and never competes with the 6 main-action turns. A player optimizing *only* for income (all 15 turns gathering/exploring/fighting) can still passively reach Way 2's Light bar for the cost of a couple of surplus Uncommon cards they were going to gift anyway (hand-limit rule already pushes them to gift excess cards). VP has no such free lane — every VP source above costs a main-action turn. This is *part* of why Way 2 came out cheaper in the arithmetic above even after deliberately compressing the VP scale, and it's the reason I can't call the 1.25x/1.5x gap "equal" with confidence. Fixing it is a `duality.json` question (systems lane owns that file), not a threshold question — e.g. capping total Care-phase Light gain per turn, or making the "gift_card" trigger require a genuinely costly card rather than any spare Uncommon. Recommend treating this as a follow-up question before the sim is extended to actually verify Way1≈Way2 numerically (the same way `montecarlo.py` turned F1 from an assertion into a solved parameter search) — I'd flag it as a new open question for Tessa to number, since Q6 asked for thresholds and this is a different, adjacent problem (the faucet feeding one side of the threshold, not the threshold itself).

## Physical feasibility

Trivial. VP scale (0–~20) fits a simple score track or tally pad — smaller than most board games' VP tracks. No hidden state, no new components beyond what a Light/Duality track already needs. Both platforms identical; no adaptation required.

## Adversarial review

**Verdict:** NEEDS_WORK

**Pillars blocked:** Balance (no dominant strategy) — Care-phase free Light lane plus the unpriced Rage-sink asymmetry between the Way1/Way2 builds mean the 1.25x-turn/1.5x-CE parity claim is not yet demonstrated to hold; author already flags the Care-phase half of this as unresolved.


Verified against GDD.md, canon/victory.json, duality.json, actions.json, rage.json, crafting.json, tiers.json, modes.json, quests.md, and `python -m sim` / `pytest -q` (both green, baseline 98.40/98.16 CE, F2 98.40→102.90→107.40 all reproduce as cited).

THE CORE INSIGHT IS RIGHT AND IS THE STRONGEST PART. The "load-bearing finding" — that `floor((VP−Light)/5)` (canon `hypocrisy_penalty`) is dead text or an always-on tax unless VP and Light share an order of magnitude — is correct, well-evidenced, and is genuinely the substance hiding under GDD Q6. The derived 1/2/4/8 VP faucet, checked against it in the "Hypocrisy Penalty check" table, actually fires sensibly (0 for ordinary players, −2 for a Way1-exact finish, 0 for Way2 since Light≥VP, exempted for Way3 via the existing `max_dark` exemption, −4 for a deliberate hypocrite). That table is correct arithmetic and a real result, not asserted balance.

But the reachability arithmetic that's supposed to demonstrate Way1≈Way2 has real problems:

1. MATH ERROR. Way2's own worked VP build: "Give-Back-Light×3 + Easy quest 1 + private goal 3 = 8 VP." Using the proposal's own new rule (give_back_light reward = +1 VP), that's 3×1 + 1 + 3 = **7, not 8**. The text then says this "comfortably clears the 7 bar" — it doesn't; it lands exactly on the bar with zero margin, the same fragility as Way1's exact-14 build, not more comfortable as claimed. This doesn't overturn the 1.25x-turns/1.5x-CE conclusion (turn counts don't depend on the sum), but it does falsify the specific claim made about it.

2. UNSOURCED NUMBER. The same build's Light total ("2 befriends + 3× Give-Back-Light + 1 creature-quest bonus = 9 Light") includes a "creature-quest bonus" that has no matching trigger in `canon/duality.json`'s `shifts` list, and the proposal's own new `creature_quest_vp` entry defines VP for creature quests, not Light. Drop it and Way2's Light is 8 — still clears ≥8, but again exactly, and the padding number should not have been there un-flagged.

3. RAGE IS COMPLETELY UNPRICED, and it isn't symmetric between the two builds. `canon/rage.json`'s `round_start` faucet adds +1 Rage every round to every player regardless of action — over a 15-turn game that's enough to reach the worst fight/discard band (`range: [9,10]`) well before endgame unless sinks are used. The only sinks are `guardian_quest_step` (−1) and `give_back_light` (−1). Way2's proposed build happens to include three `give_back_light` uses (−3 Rage) while Way1's build includes only one Guardian step (−1 Rage). The proposal never mentions Rage once, yet the two builds it's trying to equalize differ by 2 net Rage points purely as a side effect of which VP sources they picked — a real, checkable asymmetry the "comparable investment" framing ignores.

4. Way3's "Max Dark forces auto-attack" claim doesn't match canon. `duality.json` assigns "creatures auto-Bite or attack" to the **`dark`** band (range −7…−3); `max_dark` (−10…−8, where the proposed Way3 Light threshold of ≤−8 actually sits) has its own, different effect list (Corrupt Gate, double negative effects, unlocks Dark Quests) with no forced-combat clause. Invariant I4 says bands tile the track with "no gaps or overlaps," which reads as discrete non-cumulative effect sets, not inheritance from milder bands. So the 8.95-CE/turn "fight-adjusted" opportunity cost used for 2 of Way3's turns is applying a mechanic sourced from the wrong band. (Swapping it for the pacifist 8.2 CE/turn changes the total from ≈67.4 to ≈65.9 CE — doesn't overturn "slightly harder," but the justification as written is wrong.)

5. Bigger gap, not self-flagged: `max_dark`'s own effect list says it "unlocks Dark Quests" — i.e. a player must already be at −8 Duality or below *before* they can take a single Dark quest action (`dark_quest` itself only shifts Duality a further −3). Way3's "5 dedicated turns / ≈67 CE" budget prices five Dark-quest turns but budgets nothing for the descent from Duality 0 to −8 that has to happen first via `exploit_tile`/`craft_dark_kit`/`cast_dark_spell`, each of which also costs Rage (`rage.json` faucets) and, for `exploit_tile`, permanently exhausts the tile (I8: exploiting costs Light *and* raises Rage). This precondition cost is real and currently missing from the "reachability" section entirely.

INVARIANTS: No invariant test actually breaks. I4 (bands tile, no gaps) and I5 (Rage monotonic) — the two the proposal cites — remain structurally intact; the problem is the *comparison* ignores what I5 already prices (Rage), not that I5 itself is violated. `pytest -q` stays green under the current canon; the proposed edits don't touch tier ladders, deck composition, or the six-action/duality-rage trigger tables, so I1/I2/I3/I6/I7 are untouched.

PILLARS: Duality (never forced) is fine — Way 3 stays fully optional and unpenalized on its own terms. Balance (no dominant strategy) is the pillar actually at risk, and the proposal correctly self-identifies its biggest threat: `care` phase is `bonus_only`/`costs_action:false` (`actions.json`), so `gift_card`/`generous_trade_3ce` Light gains are free relative to Way1's VP-and-Light-bundled quest turns. Combined with `befriend_creature` already being folded into the ordinary baseline income mix (F1: "2 creatures outer, always befriended"), Way2's Light bar may be nearly ambient/free while its VP bar takes ~3-4 turns — versus Way1 needing both VP and Light from the same 5 dedicated turns. That's a real risk to "no dominant strategy," and to the proposal's credit it says so explicitly and declines to claim victory, recommending a quest-aware sim extension before calling Way1≈Way2 verified. That's the right call, though I'd add: it also needs to account for the Rage asymmetry (#3 above) and the Way3 precondition cost (#5) before the sim work starts, since those change what "equal treatment" would even mean to test for.

PHYSICAL FEASIBILITY: trivial, no objection. A 0–20ish VP track is smaller than most published games' score tracks; no new hidden state or component burden.

DEGENERATE PLAY: No infinite loop or dead option found. The nearest thing to an exploit is the free Care-phase Light lane already flagged above/by the author — real, but not yet provably "dominant" without a sim that prices actual VP-quest availability per turn, which the author correctly says doesn't exist yet.

Net: the scale-matching insight and the Hypocrisy-Penalty verification are sound and should survive to the next draft. The specific 14/7/16 numbers and the "comparable investment" claim supporting them rest on arithmetic that has one outright error (7 vs 8), one unsourced padding term, one wrong band citation, and two unpriced cost dimensions (Rage asymmetry, Way3's Dark-unlock precondition) that weren't in the risk list. None of this breaks a named invariant or a pillar outright, but it does mean "same order of magnitude, verified by hand" is currently overstated — the honest current state is closer to "same order of magnitude, plausibly, pending a sim that also accounts for Rage and the Dark-quest unlock cost."
