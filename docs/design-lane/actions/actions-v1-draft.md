# Actions — v1 Draft (Care + Core Actions)

> First sketch of all action descriptions. Raw draft for reference.

## Care Phase: Eat / Sleep / Meditate / Trade (Bonus-Only)

The Care Phase happens at the start of a player's turn. It is bonus-only and does not consume Action Points (AP).

- **Consume Food/Potions:** Eat gathered food or drink potions to restore Energy (up to a cap of 5). Potions are single-use consumables and can *only* be drank during this phase.
- **Sleep:** Skip the Action Phase entirely to gain `+2 Energy`.
- **Meditate:** Spend `1 Energy` (or use a Meditation tile) to gain focus. This unlocks the **Learning** action for the current turn.
- **Trade & Gift:** Conduct open trades with any player. 
  * *Duality aspect:* Gifting a card (or trading with a net value disparity of `≥3 CE` in their favor) instantly awards the giver `+1 Light`.

## Main Actions (Choose 1)

A player selects exactly one main action during the Action Phase.

### 1. Move / Explore / Gather
- **Move:** Move a distance up to the player's current Move Speed across explored tiles.
- **Explore:** If movement ends on or crosses a face-down (unexplored) tile, end movement, flip it face-up, and draw 1 Event Card + 1 Resource Card from that ring's deck.
- **Gather:** If movement ends on an explored tile, gather resource elements:
  * *Outer Ring (T1):* Gain 2 Commons of that tile's element + 1 draw from the T1 Resource deck.
  * *Inner Ring (T2):* Gain 2 Commons + draw 2 keep 1 from the T2 Resource deck.
  * *Duality aspect:* Exploiting a tile's resource (gathering double yield but exhausting the tile) instantly gives `+1 Dark`.

### 2. Craft
- **Craft Item:** Use recipes to craft tools, gear, weapons, or consumables.
  * *Benches:* Uncommon/Rare items require a player to be on or adjacent to a Crafting Bench or Forge. Legendary items require a bench and a player-built Workshop.
  * *Duality aspect:* Crafting a **Dark Kit** item (e.g., tripwire, snare trap) instantly gives `+1 Dark`. Crafting a **Spirit Item** (e.g., prayer ribbon, censer) instantly gives `+1 Light`.

### 3. Magic
- **Cast Spell:** Activate a character signature ability or a magic spell learned from a Skill Book.
  * *Duality aspect:* Spells tagged as Dark (e.g., draining energy from a rival) instantly give `-2 Light`. Spells tagged as Light (e.g., healing a rival) instantly give `+1 Light`.

### 4. Learning
- **Learn Skill:** Unlock a new node on the character's skill tree or learn from a found Skill Book.
  * *Prerequisite:* Requires meditation during the Care Phase of the current turn.
  * *Cost:* Pay resources matching the skill's tier (Common skill = 3 CE, Uncommon = 6 CE, Rare = 18 CE).

### 5. Quest
- **Complete Quest:** Fulfill requirements for Common, Guardian, or Creature quests on your current tile.
  * *Duality aspect:* Fulfilling a Guardian's *Give-back* requirement instantly gives `+2 Light`. Fulfilling a Dark Quest (e.g., desecrating a shrine) instantly gives `+3 Dark`.

### 6. Give Back Light (Wild Card / Supporting Action)
- **Altruistic Action:** Spend resources to perform actions that benefit other players.
  * *Acts:* Building a shared Crafting Bench or Bridge for everyone to use, placing a card in the Storage Cache, or helping another player fulfill a Group Quest.
  * *Duality aspect:* Instantly gives `+2 Light` and a small VP reward.
