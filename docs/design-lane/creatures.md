# Creatures — Into the Wild

> Draft roster (2026-07-06) — **100 creatures**. Not balanced, not simulated.
> Card chassis: **Demand** · **Gift** (Light reaction) · **Bite** (Dark reaction AND fight-loss effect) · **F** (fight number) · **Quirk** · **Companion perk**.

## Encounter Rules (chassis)

- **Befriend — no dice.** Meet the **Demand** → befriend: 1 ring draw + 1 Light + a **favor token** on the creature. Kindness is always available at a price.
- **Fight — the only gamble.** Roll 1d6 + affinity/gear ≥ **F** → win: 2 loot draws (Dark lean). Lose → suffer the **Bite**. No HP — Bites are setbacks only (spec §6).
- **Light-band reactions:** Light band → the **Gift** happens on meeting. Dark band → the **Bite** happens. **Neutral band** → the creature offers its Demand as a challenge: meet it (+1 Light) or exploit it (+1 Dark, +1 draw, creature leaves angry).
- **Temper / companion track:** favor tokens persist on the creature card (it stays in play near its tile). **3 favors → companion**: travels with you, perk active. **Max 1 companion**; swap by releasing (released creature keeps 1 favor). Legendaries usually grant a permanent **title** instead of travelling.
- **♥ / ✗ affinity:** each creature loves one element affinity (♥: −1 F, first Demand halved where sensible) and distrusts one (✗: +1 F, cannot become your companion).
- **F guide by tier:** C 3–4 · U 4–5 · R 5–7 · L 7–9.
- **Quests:** Rare/Legendary creatures carry a printed quest hook (listed under each table).

- **Bond webs:** several creatures reference each other (Parrot↔Whistler feud, Calf→Last Leviathan, Mirrorback→Pale Stag). Seed more cross-references in testing.
- **Table-rule Demands** (hum, bow, push-ups, straight face) are the liveliness engine — physical-build gold; digital replaces with mini-prompts.

- **Dark servicing:** Sulfur Imp, Nightshade Bat, Voidcap Shade, Hollow King give Way 3 its own creature economy.
