# Character: The Botanist

## Profile & Traits
- **Background:** Ship's physician and naturalist, deeply connected to flora, potions, and organic growth.
- **Element Affinity:** ♥ Wood/Earth (Jungle), ✗ Moon (Swamp)
- **Base Move Speed:** Average.
- **Unique Signature Item:** *Herbalist's Mortar* — Unique craftable tool. Can brew restorative potions at any campfire without needing an Apothecary Hut building.

## Unique Skill
- **Flora Whispering:**
  * Concept: Can encourage faster resource regeneration on explored tiles, extract medicinal properties, or identify hidden toxins.

## Bonuses & Perks
- **Bountiful Harvest:** Gains double yield when gathering organic/plant resources (leaves, flowers, herbs).
- **Thorn Immunity:** Unaffected by traps or hazards made from jungle thorns or vines.
- **Herbal Pocket:** Organic/plant advanced resource cards (herbs, flowers, sap, spores) do not count toward their 7-card hand limit.

## Skill Tree Draft

### Generic Branch: Survivalist
*Shared with all castaways.*
1. **Scavenger's Eye (Common):** Gain +1 Common resource of choice when gathering.
2. **Sturdy Pack (Uncommon):** +2 backpack slots (increases carrying capacity from 6 to 8).
3. **Iron Constitution (Rare):** Meditating at camp costs 0 energy (free meditation).

### Unique Branch: Herbalist
*Unique to the Botanist.*
1. **Sap Extraction (Common):** Convert wood/jungle commons into energy or basic medicine.
2. **Growth Surge (Uncommon):** Choose an explored tile; double its resource yields for all players on the next round.
3. **Toxin Cleansing (Rare):** Instantly cure poison setbacks for yourself or any player on your tile.
