# Character: The Blacksmith

## Profile & Traits
- **Background:** Ship's carpenter and metalworker, expert in structures, tool forging, and structural integrity.
- **Element Affinity:** ♥ Fire (Mountain/Volcano), ✗ Water/Sky (Lake/Beach)
- **Base Move Speed:** Low.
- **Unique Signature Item:** *Pocket Bellows* — Unique craftable tool. Speeds up metalwork recipes and allows basic smelting at campfires (bypassing the Forge requirement for simple tools).

## Unique Skill
- **Metal Tempering:**
  * Concept: Can repair damaged gear on the fly, reinforce active tools, or strengthen shared player buildings against weather/events.

## Bonuses & Perks
- **Durable Construction:** Crafted tools start with extra durability or can be used twice before needing repairs.
- **Deep Miner:** Yields bonus metal ores (copper, iron, tin) when gathering on Mountain/Volcano tiles.
- **Alloy Smelter:** Performs bank conversions (up-grading metals) at a 2:1 ratio (instead of 4:1) at any Crafting Bench, and smelis ores at buildings for -1 Common material.

## Skill Tree Draft

### Generic Branch: Survivalist
*Shared with all castaways.*
1. **Scavenger's Eye (Common):** Gain +1 Common resource of choice when gathering.
2. **Sturdy Pack (Uncommon):** +2 backpack slots (increases carrying capacity from 6 to 8).
3. **Iron Constitution (Rare):** Meditating at camp costs 0 energy (free meditation).

### Unique Branch: Metalworking
*Unique to the Blacksmith.*
1. **Tool Reinforcement (Common):** Upgrade a common tool to grant +1 to resource gathering checks.
2. **Alloy Smelting (Uncommon):** Combine basic metal ores into stronger alloys without wasting resources.
3. **Earthshaping (Rare):** Dig tunnels through impassable mountain borders, creating permanent paths for all players.
