# Character: The Cartographer

## Profile & Traits
- **Background:** Former ship's navigator, obsessed with charts, mapping the unknown, and finding paths.
- **Element Affinity:** ♥ Water/Sky (Lake/Beach), ✗ Fire (Mountain/Volcano)
- **Base Move Speed:** High base mobility.
- **Unique Signature Item:** *Brass Sextant* — Unique craftable tool. Allows the player to re-path their movement after a face-down tile event is revealed.

## Unique Skill
- **Mapmaker's Sight:**
  * Concept: Can spend energy to survey the surrounding island terrain, peeking at face-down hex tiles to plan routes or bypass obstacles.

## Bonuses & Perks
- **Pathfinding:** Moves faster and consumes less energy when travelling over fully revealed/explored tiles.
- **Landmark Discovery:** Gains extra resources when discovering named landmarks or special tiles.
- **Foresight:** Immune to the card-discard penalty imposed by high Island Rage (Rage 5+) during creature Bites or Event setbacks.

## Skill Tree Draft

### Generic Branch: Survivalist
*Shared with all castaways.*
1. **Scavenger's Eye (Common):** Gain +1 Common resource of choice when gathering.
2. **Sturdy Pack (Uncommon):** +2 backpack slots (increases carrying capacity from 6 to 8).
3. **Iron Constitution (Rare):** Meditating at camp costs 0 energy (free meditation).

### Unique Branch: Pathfinder
*Unique to the Cartographer.*
1. **Horizon Scan (Common):** Peek at the type (color/element) of 1 adjacent face-down tile without entering it.
2. **Swift Traverse (Uncommon):** Ignore movement penalties on water and beach tiles.
3. **Leyline Shortcut (Rare):** Teleport between any two explored Shrine or Moonwell tiles by spending energy.
