# Weapons & Wards — Into the Wild

> Status: design draft. Weapons aid the **fight** route (2 loot draws, Dark lean); wards aid **befriend/protect** (Light lean). Same category, mirrored — the choice is the Duality expression.
> Progression: C/U outer ring · R inner ring · L needs Crafting Bench.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Notes**
- Fight vs. befriend EV stays per economy §3.3; weapons/wards shift odds, not the Light/Dark accounting.
- Ward crafting uses Spirit resources → wards are earned, not just bought. Fits "Spirit = earned" (spec §15).
