# Buildings & Benches — Into the Wild

> Status: design draft. Buildings are **shared** — anyone on the tile may use them (economy §4.1: building = Give-Back-Light play). Permanent.
> Progression: C camps turn 1 · U stations turns 3–6 · R benches (the crafting gate) turns 5–10 · L wonders turn 10+.

| Item | Element | Tier | CE | Recipe | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Campfire | Fire | C | 3 | 2 Sticks + 1 Flint | Anyone on tile may cook meals |
| Lean-to | Wood | C | 3 | 2 Leaves + 1 Stick | End turn here: +1 ⚡ |
| Fish Smoker | Water | U | 6 | 1 Salt Crystal (U) + 3 Sticks | Meals cooked here give +1 ⚡ extra |
| Herb Garden | Wood | U | 6 | 1 Fibrous Root (U) + 2 Clay + 1 Straw | Once per round: first player here takes 1 Common (Leaves or Grain) |
| **Crafting Bench** | Wood | R | 18 | 1 Ironwood (R) + 1 Hardwood (U) + 6 Commons (Sticks/Stone/Vines) | **Required** for Legendary crafts and up-conversion (economy §4.2) |
| Spirit Shrine | Spirit | R | 18 | 1 Sacred Relic (R) + 1 Blessing (U) + 6 Commons (Spirit Mote/Incense/Stone) | Meditate here: +1 Light **or** +1 Spirit Mote |
| World Tree Sapling | Wood | L | 54 | 1 Eternal Sapling (L) + 1 Heartwood (R) + 1 Ancient Seed (R) + 9 Commons | Each round every player gains 1 Common of choice; builder +2 Light |
| Beacon Tower | Sky | L | 54 | 1 Skyfont (L) + 1 Stormglass (R) + 1 Fire Crystal (R) + 9 Commons | Reveals all tiles within 2; once per round a player on its ring may end their move here |

**Notes**
- The Crafting Bench is the tech gate of the whole item system: no bench → no Legendary items, no up-conversion. Building it publicly is a strong kind act; a Dark player building a private one is a story moment — TBD whether Dark players can restrict access.
- L buildings are group engines — they reward everyone, builder gets Light. This is the building category's core identity: **buildings are the most Light-flavored sink in the game.**
