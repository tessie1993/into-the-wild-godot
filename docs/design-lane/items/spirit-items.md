# Spirit Items — Into the Wild

> Status: design draft. Spirit resources are **earned, not gathered** (spec §15) — so every item here is progression-gated by behavior, not luck.
> Spirit-gated recipes additionally require a minimum Light level (marked ✦).

| Item | Tier | CE | Recipe | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prayer Braid | C | 3 | 2 Spirit Mote + 1 Vines | Gift it: both players +1 Light (once) |
| Incense Bundle | C | 3 | 2 Incense + 1 Straw | Your meditation also yields +1 Spirit Mote |
| Faith Talisman | U | 6 | 1 Faith Token (U) + 1 Candleflame (U) | Reroll one Duality-phase world reaction |
| Echo Stone | U | 6 | 1 Echo (U) + 3 Stone | Repeat your last Care-phase kind act for free |
| Prayer Beads | R | 18 | 1 Prayer Bead (R) + 1 Spirit Essence (U) + 6 Commons (Spirit Mote/Incense) | World reactions treat your Light as one level higher |
| Offering Bowl | R | 18 | 1 Aura Crystal (R) + 1 Blessing (U) + 6 Commons | Guardian quest give-back costs reduced by 3 CE |
| Guardian's Cradle ✦ | L | 54 | 1 Pure Light† (L) + 2 Rares + 9 Commons — **crafting requires gifting a Rare** | Revive/shield any player once |
| Worldsoul Locket ✦ | L | 54 | 1 Worldsoul Fragment (L) + 1 Soul Ember† (R) + 1 Guardian's Favor (R) + 9 Commons (Spirit Mote) | Once per game: all players +1 Light |
| Master's Sigil ✦ | L | 54 | 1 Master's Blessing (L) + 1 Sacred Relic (R) + 1 Prayer Bead (R) + 9 Commons | Offering piece for the Master Guardian challenge; +2 on that challenge |

**Notes**
- ✦ = Spirit-gated: craftable only at high Light (exact threshold TBD with Path of Duality track values).
- This category is the Way-2 (Enlightened Path) engine: Braid → Talisman → Beads → Locket is a Light-acceleration chain.
- Prayer Beads + medium Light effectively rescues players from the "medium is worst" trough — intended ladder out, playtest the strength.
