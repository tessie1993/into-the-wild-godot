# Dark Kit — Into the Wild

> Status: design draft. Built from Fork resources' dark side (resources.md §A).
> **Light cost on use, not craft:** crafting is free; each dark use = −1 Light (economy §7). Fork items list both outputs — the choice at use is the mechanic.
> Progression: C/U outer ring (swamp-heavy) · R inner ring · L max-Dark endgame.

| Item | Element | Tier | CE | Recipe | Light use | Dark use (−1 Light) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thorn Caltrops | Wood | C | 3 | 2 Sticks + 1 Vines | — | Rival entering your tile loses 1 move |
| Smoke Pot | Ether | C | 3 | 1 Peat + 1 Spores + 1 Moss | Flee a creature without resolving its event | Cover an ally's flee too? No — flee is neutral; hiding *from a player* is dark |
| Poison Vial | Ether | U | 6 | 1 Nightshade† (U) + 1 Swamp Gas (U) | Antidote: cleanse poison from anyone | Coat weapon (+2 fight) OR spoil a rival's food (−2 ⚡) |
| Bog-Iron Shiv | Ether | U | 6 | 1 Bog Iron (U) + 1 Venom Sac† (U) | — | Raid: steal 1 Common from a player on your tile |
| Bloodvine Snare | Wood | R | 18 | 1 Bloodvine† (R) + 1 Resin (U) + 6 Commons | Heal-splint (restore an ally) | Rob: take 1 card from a player on your tile |
| Shadowmoss Cloak | Ether | R | 18 | 1 Shadowmoss† (R) + 1 Witchmoss (U) + 6 Commons (Moss/Spores) | Camouflage: dodge a creature | Ambush: your position hidden until you act (digital: literal hidden state; physical: token off-board, tile noted secretly) |
| Voidcap Mask | Ether | L | 54 | 1 Voidcap† (L) + 2 Rares + 9 Commons | — | Hide your Light level (flip track marker). **Aligned:** corrupts Light holders |
| Crown of Brimstone | Fire | L | 54 | 1 Phoenix Ember† (L) + 1 Brimstone† (R) + 1 Magma Glass (R) + 9 Commons (Coal/Stone/Spores) | — | **Max-Dark only.** Raids hit 2 targets at once — the Way-3 signature item |

**Notes**
- Items with a Light-use column are safe to craft for anyone (fork stays open); "—" items are dark-only — carrying them is a tell.
- Way-3 spine: Shiv → Snare → Cloak → Crown. Total kit ≈ 132 CE — a full-game commitment, consistent with "full dark + very selfish" (spec §6).
- Physical hidden-position rule (Shadowmoss Cloak) needs a clean tabletop implementation — flag for prototype.
